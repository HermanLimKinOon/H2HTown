# Local Blender portrait study v2

Mirror-only approximation of the user-provided character illustration. No Meshy model or cloud generation was used. This is not an exact reconstruction.

Anatomical starting topology, skeleton, fitted clothes and shoes derive from the existing MakeHuman Community CC0-based mirror character. Original sources and credits remain in mirror-sculpted.LICENSE.md, mirror-reference.LICENSE.md and mirror-mpfb.LICENSE.md.

This revision reshapes the face, eyelids, brows and chin; replaces projected facial image colours with procedural vertex-colour skin and lip shading; fits a continuous sculpted hair surface; and adds cream soles and green sneaker panels. The mountain shirt print derives from user-provided artwork; no CC0 claim is made for that artwork or its derivatives.

Editable source: assets/characters/blender-reference-v2.blend.
Builder: scripts/sculpt-reference-portrait.py.
Game export: public/game3d/blender-reference-v2.glb.
The editable source retains the detailed hair; the game export uses a reduced surface with facial and physique morphs retained.

Draft key: h2h-blender-reference-preview-v2. Applying this preview to the world is disabled. The v1 study and world character are preserved separately.
